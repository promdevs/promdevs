- Verified: 2026-09-16. Metadata reports public_domain: true.

The bundled head is cropped, centered and rescaled from the museum STL using
scripts/assets/prepare-apollo.mjs. The original is not shipped. The torso,
articulated arms, hands and workstation are original procedural geometry.
The poster is rendered from this same adapted scene. No paid assets are used.
